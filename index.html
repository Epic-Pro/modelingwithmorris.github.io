I build financial models and valuations for people making big business decisions.

I'm Morris, a freelance financial modeler in Nairobi. Most of my work is helping deal teams, founders and business owners work out what a company is worth, and whether a deal, a raise or a budget actually makes sense.

I mainly work on M&A and LBO models and valuation, and I do FP&A forecasting for companies that need a better handle on their numbers.

Some of my work

  • Valuation for an investment decision A confidential electronics company, screened for a possible acquisition using a DCF and comparable companies, ending in a buy or sell view.
  • SaaS financial model MRR and ARR forecast, cost structure, cash runway in months, and unit metrics: CAC, ARPA and LTV/CAC.
  • FP&A forecast and budget A driver-based forecast with an income statement and three scenarios. The expense sheet changes when you switch the scenario.
  • Three-statement model Income statement, balance sheet and cash flow for a demo company, with break-even analysis and a trial balance.

Client names and numbers stay private. If you'd like to see how a model is built, ask and I'll walk you through a sample.

What I can help with

  • Buy-side and sell-side M&A models, LBO analysis and returns
  • Company valuation: DCF, comparable companies, precedent transactions
  • Cap tables and exit waterfalls
  • Forecasts, budgets and scenario planning
  • Models for SaaS and early-stage companies
  • Automating the monthly reporting that eats up your team's time

About me

I've been doing this for over eight years, and I've built more than 900 models for clients in Kenya, Saudi Arabia and a few other places. On Upwork I'm Top Rated with a 100% Job Success Score.

I care more about a model being understood than being impressive. If you can't follow the assumptions, you can't trust the answer, so I keep the structure plain and the inputs easy to change.
